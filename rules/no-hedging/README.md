Catches the sentence that hedges the same claim three times over, and the known behaviour softened into something that might in certain cases occur. The regex-grade neighbour is the hedge word list, `may`, `might`, `could`, `possibly`, `generally`, `typically`, `in some cases`, which a `grep -inE` or Vale's `write-good.Weasel` will count for free; counting is the part grep does well and deciding is the part it cannot, because one `may` on a dependency's behaviour is honest and the same word stacked on `potentially` is the fault.
