Catches the sentence before the first fact and the section after the last one: a heading restated as a paragraph, a warning that the topic is important, a chatbot's acknowledgement of the request, and a closing Summary that repeats the page. The regex-grade neighbour is the phrase list, `Great question`, `Certainly`, `I hope this helps`, `Let's dive in`, and the heading pattern `^#+ (Summary|Conclusion|TL;DR|Final thoughts)`, both of which a `grep -inE` in a pre-commit hook settles; what it cannot settle is whether the section under that heading restates the page or adds the caveat the body owed the reader, which is the whole of this rule.
