Catches the range whose two ends are not on a scale, the feature list dressed as a span: everything from your linter config to your changelog, integrations from Slack to Postgres. The regex-grade neighbour is the construction itself, `grep -nE 'everything from .+ to '`, or a Vale phrase rule; grep finds every `from X to Y` in the page and cannot tell the version range, the port range and the maintenance window from the two items that were only ever a list, which is the call this rule makes.
