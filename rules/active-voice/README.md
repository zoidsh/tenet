Catches the passive that hides a doer the page has already named, and the impersonal frame that reports a decision nobody made. The regex-grade neighbour is the passive detector itself, `grep -nE '\b(is|are|was|were|be|been) [a-z]+ed\b'` or Vale's `write-good.Passive`, which finds every passive on the page including the ones the tenet explicitly allows; the tenet's exception is the whole difficulty, and deciding whether the actor is unknown or merely unmentioned is what this rule is for.
