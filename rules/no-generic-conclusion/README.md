Catches the roadmap that promises nothing and the status section that says the project is evolving: a closing whose sentences cannot be kept or broken because they name no version, no issue and no date. The regex-grade neighbour is the stock phrase, `watch this space`, `the future looks bright`, `stay tuned`, `exciting things ahead`, which a `grep -inF -f phrases.txt` in a pre-commit hook will catch; the paragraph that avoids all of them and still promises nothing is what is left, and it is what this rule reads.
