Catches a comment that says what the code already says: a step narrated beside the statement that performs it, a section label, or a doc comment that spells the identifier out in other words. It leaves the mechanical half to a static linter, which is better at whether an exported identifier has a doc comment at all, at its first word, and at a stale `//nolint` directive; what is left is the judgement of whether a reader learns anything from the sentence.
