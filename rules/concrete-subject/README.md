Catches the sentence where a quality does the acting: simplicity drives the design, the architecture cares about correctness, the roadmap wants plugins. The regex-grade neighbour is the nominalisation check, `grep -nE '^[A-Z][a-z]+(tion|ment|ity|ance|ness) '` or Vale's `write-good.Nominalizations`, which finds abstract nouns in the subject position and stops there; a rising latency and a growing cost are abstract subjects doing nothing they cannot do, and separating those from an ideal that decides things is the judgement this rule makes.
