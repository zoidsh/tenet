Add discount codes to invoice totals

Sales has been giving discounts over the phone and
keying the reduced total in by hand, which is where
most of this month's invoice corrections came from.
A code on the invoice carries the discount itself.

Rounding goes to the house: half a cent of discount
is dropped rather than given away, because the
ledger reconciles in whole cents.

A two-way door. The field is additive and nothing
outside DiscountedTotalCents reads it, so reverting
this commit walks the change back.

Look at the rounding in DiscountedTotalCents first.
